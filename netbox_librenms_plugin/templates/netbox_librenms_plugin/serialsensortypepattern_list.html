Serial Sensor Types

Console servers expose their serial lines as LibreNMS state sensors, not interface-table rows. Each entry here makes one vendor's sensor_type recognized by the Cables tab's serial console-port sync and names the matching local ConsoleServerPorts — {N} is replaced by the sensor's port number.

Ships with Avocent ACS (acsSerialPortTable → ttyS{N}) and Cisco IOS async lines (ciscoAsyncLine → Line {N}). Add a row to surface a new vendor; delete a row to stop recognizing one — matching is exact, including case.
